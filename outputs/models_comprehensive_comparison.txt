KEY DIFFERENTIATING TAKEAWAYS:
1. Raw Gaussian Noisy Input Baseline (σ = 0.3): MSE = 0.04665, PSNR = 13.32 dB.
2. Undercomplete AE achieves the highest clean reconstruction fidelity (23.06 dB) and best linear separability,
   but fails abruptly when evaluated on noisy inputs (drops to 12.82 dB, worse than raw noise).
3. Denoising AE achieves remarkable noise robustness: cleans noisy inputs from 13.32 dB to 21.63 dB (+8.31 dB gain)
   while preserving strong representation accuracy.
4. Variational AE trades a small amount of pixel-level fidelity (19.90 dB) for probabilistic regularity:
   its latent space conforms to N(0, I), enabling valid generative prior sampling and smooth manifold interpolation.
